Open construction bids

Solicitations from NYC agencies that are open for bids now, soonest due first. These are public jobs: anyone who is qualified can bid. Click a bid for the scope, the agency contact and the link to the full notice.

How it works

Every morning the page reads the City Record, the city's official notice board, and keeps the solicitations in the two construction categories whose due date has not passed. A correction notice replaces the earlier notice for the same PIN. Contacts are the agency contacts the city lists; some agencies list none and take bids only through PASSPort, so search the PIN there.

"Matches my trades" is a keyword match on the title and scope, so it can miss a bid or catch one that is not for you. Use "All construction bids" to see everything, and always read the full notice before you bid. A general contractor sees every bid.

Source: NYC Open Data, City Record Online, updated every morning.
