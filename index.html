Why it's early

Most lead services show you a job once the permit is issued. By then, it's been bought out.

Big projects have to file with the public long before a shovel goes in. In Westchester, every town must send larger site plans to the County Planning Board. In NYC, every job is filed with the Department of Buildings. Contract Connect watches both every day, so you hear about it at the first public step.

Questions

FAQ

Where does the data come from?

Westchester County Planning Board referrals, which every municipality must file for larger land-use cases, and NYC Department of Buildings job filings and permits. Both are public records. We pull them every morning and do the sorting, sizing and research for you.

How early is "early"?

A planning referral or DOB filing usually comes months before a building permit, often before the owner has picked a GC or bought out the trades.

How accurate are the dollar estimates?

They're for deciding which jobs to chase, not for bidding. They use the project's floor area, a cost per square foot you can adjust, and typical cost shares for each trade.

What kinds of projects are included?

Private projects with a real building in them: roughly 5,000 square feet or more, or 5 or more homes. Very large projects that a local builder is unlikely to win are left off the call list but still searchable.

Can I change my areas later?

Yes. Turn towns and boroughs on or off whenever you like from your account.
